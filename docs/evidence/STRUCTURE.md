# Structure

## Established

The 17 designed mutation sites are in the LBD. Structural analysis supports a long-range pocket ↔ HTH relationship, with the ligand pocket and DNA-binding region separated by roughly 33 Å in the analyzed models.

Q164R is located at the ligand-pocket wall in the project structure analysis.

## Interpretation boundary

Structure supports mechanistic hypotheses and candidate prioritization. It does not replace paired genotype × ligand phenotype measurements.
