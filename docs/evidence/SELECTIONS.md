# Bile-acid selections

## Established observation

Q164R is enriched across all six bile-acid selection pools and lies at the ligand-pocket wall.

This is a pooled-selection frequency and structural observation. It does not by itself measure genotype-specific activity or prove a universal response switch.

## Data context

- Six bile-acid selection pools
- Nanopore population sequencing
- 17 designed LBD sites
- Round-0/lib0 baseline is not yet available for complete enrichment correction

## Interpretation boundary

Use: **shared mutation across six selections, with structural evidence supporting a role in ligand recognition.**

Do not use yet: **universal response switch** or ligand-specific causal effect.

## Next validation

Measure matched genotypes across ligands with baseline, ± ligand response, dose response, and replicates.
